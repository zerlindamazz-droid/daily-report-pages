Yield Gap Thesis: Attacking Wealth Inequality via Returns, Not Just Income
🇨🇳 中文解读
Saylor指出"收益差"（yield gap）是被忽视的财富分化根源：穷人因持币少而获利低，进而缺乏存钱动力，加速消费贫困化。$STRC通过让小额持有者也能获得机构级收益，直接攻击这个问题。这是一个"市场失灵"的诊断——传统金融无法服务小额用户的风险定价成本过高。
🇬🇧 English Breakdown
The yield gap mechanism: lower wealth → lower returns → weaker savings incentive → faster wealth erosion. $STRC directly addresses this by delivering institutional-grade yields to retail holders. This diagnoses a market failure: traditional finance's cost structure makes sub-$100K accounts unprofitable to serve.
